Flexible Work Timesheet

Sharper visibility, more controls, and auto-jump to the correct week every time you open the file.

This version automatically jumps to the current week on open, while keeping older weeks saved.
How to use
  1. Step 1: Choose a week, or click Current week.
  2. Step 2: Pick a status for each day.
  3. Step 3: Set Start, then use Use proposed to fill Finish quickly.
  4. Step 4: Open Settings to control hours, interval, density, visibility, and more.
  5. Step 5: Use Clear to wipe one day only.
