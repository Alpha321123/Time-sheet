Flexible Work Timesheet

A polished, standalone timesheet you can upload directly to GitHub Pages. It shows a fixed Monday–Friday week, calculates proposed finish times as soon as you enter your start time, adjusts Friday to hit the weekly target, auto-saves in your browser, and supports one-click clock in and clock out rounded to the nearest 15 minutes.
