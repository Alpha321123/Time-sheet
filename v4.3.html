How to use
  1. Step 1: Choose your week at the top, or move with Previous and Next Week.
  2. Step 2: For each day, pick a Status. Use Work for normal days, or choose Leave, Public holiday, or Don’t want to work.
  3. Step 3: Set your Start time or use the quick buttons / Clock in. The tool immediately calculates your Proposed finish.
  4. Step 4: Lunch is shown clearly between start and finish. You can change the lunch minutes in Settings; the default is 30 minutes.
  5. Step 5: Add any daily notes you want. Use Clear to wipe one day without touching the rest of the week.
  6. Step 6: On Friday, watch the Friday plan and Friday target work tiles.
Tip: The live Current time and Today remaining tiles help you judge your day at a glance.
Print: Use Print summary for a clean weekly printout.
